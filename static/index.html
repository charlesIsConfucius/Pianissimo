    How to find your Google Calendar Feed URL

    1. Open Google Calendar on your desktop (calendar.google.com).
    2. On the left sidebar, find your calendar under "My calendars".
    3. Click the three dots (⋮) next to your calendar → select "Settings and sharing".
    4. Scroll down to the section named "Integrate calendar".
    5. Copy the link in "Secret address in iCal format" (ends with .ics).
    6. Paste it into Pianissimo and click "Sync Feed"!
